Nothing Bundt Cakes Brings Back Biscoff Cookie Butter Favorite Alongside Galactic Brownie and Apple Pie Flavors

August 11, 2026 · ·

Nothing Bundt Cakes is helping sweet tooths embrace autumn routines with a limited-time lineup featuring the return of its popular Biscoff Cookie Butter Cake, plus two brand-new Pop-Up Bundtlet creations: Galactic Brownie Cake and Apple Pie Cake. Designed for back-to-school milestones and autumn get-togethers, the sweet collection offers something for every gathering. It also anchors the brand’s “10 Million Bites of Joy” campaign, a six-week movement running from Aug. 24 through Oct. 4.

Seasonal Flavors and Limited-Time Pop-Up Creations

Guests can look forward to three distinct offerings rolling out across the calendar:

Biscoff Cookie Butter Cake (Aug. 17 – Oct. 11): This returning favorite features warm spice cake with real Biscoff Cookie Butter baked directly inside and drizzled over the top. It comes finished with crunchy Biscoff cookies on both the top and bottom, available in all cake sizes while supplies last.

Galactic Brownie Cake (Aug. 24 – Sept. 6): A fresh Pop-Up Bundtlet option that pairs fudgy chocolate cake with a rich brownie batter glaze and colorful candy-coated rainbow sprinkles. Available exclusively in individual Bundtlet sizes while supplies last.

Apple Pie Cake (Sept. 14 – Sept. 27): This cozy Pop-Up Bundtlet incorporates warmly spiced cake packed with sweet cinnamon apples and crowned with a buttery brown sugar streusel topping. Available solely as a Bundtlet while supplies last.

“As families settle into new routines and begin gathering for the season ahead, we’re happy to welcome back a fan favorite while introducing something new for Guests to discover,” said Dolf Berle, CEO of Nothing Bundt Cakes. “At Nothing Bundt Cakes, we believe joy is meant to be shared, and the return of Biscoff Cookie Butter Cake alongside two exciting new Pop-Up Flavors gives Guests even more delicious ways to do just that, whether they’re celebrating a back-to-school milestone, reconnecting with loved ones or simply treating themselves.”

Early Rewards Access and Direct Ordering

The seasonal collection will be available across Nothing Bundt Cakes locations nationwide for a limited run. Guests can stop by local bakeries in person, order online, or place orders through the brand’s mobile app. Members enrolled in the free Bundtastic Rewards program receive early access to purchase the new Pop-Up Flavors ahead of general availability.

To locate a nearby bakery or order online for upcoming fall celebrations, head over to NothingBundtCakes.com. If you’re interested in learning more about franchising, check out NothingBundtCakes.com/franchise-opportunities/.
