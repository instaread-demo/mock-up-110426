Days of Our Lives Spoilers Wednesday, November 4: Sophia’s Three Shockers, Xander’s Secret Fear, Brady Pressures Sarah

Days of Our Lives (DOOL) spoilers for Wednesday, November 4, reveal that Sophia Choi (Rachel Boyd) has multiple curveballs coming up.

Xander Kiriakis (Paul Telfer) will also make Marlena Evans (Deidre Hall) his confidant and perhaps admit something he’s afraid of.

Elsewhere, Brady Black (Eric Martsolf) will push Sarah Horton (Linsey Godfrey) to be totally honest with herself, so here’s what DOOL has in store.

First, let’s discuss Sophia since it looks like she’ll lurk around town in a Day of the Dead costume.

Days spoilers say Chanel Dupree DiMera (Raven Bowens) will have a surprise run-in with someone, so it could be Sophia while she’s wearing her mask.

Sophia will slip up later by letting Holly Jonas (Ashley Puzemis) catch a glimpse of her face.

Although Sophia will put her mask back on quickly, she won’t do it fast enough to stop Holly from recognizing her.

Days spoilers say Sophia will rush back to her motel room, where she’ll meet up with Kristen DiMera (Stacy Haiduk).

Kristen has the money that Sophia has been demanding and will be ready to make their final transaction.

Although Kristen may insist there’s no reason for Sophia to stick around Salem now, Sophia will struggle to leave everyone behind again.

Part of Sophia will wish she could just undo all her mistakes and stay in town, so Kristen will totally understand that.

That’s what Kristen wishes she could do as well, so she’ll connect with Sophia and catch her off guard in Wednesday’s DOOL episode.

Between Kristen’s compassion and Sophia’s earlier encounters, she has three shockers on the way.

In the meantime, Brady will speak with Sarah about the Xander situation.

Days viewers know Sarah and Brady split up because she’s still in love with Xander, so Brady may get confirmation that their reunion is official.

Xander and Sarah are indeed back together, but Brady may think Sarah needs to be realistic about Xander’s flaws.

Trouble seems to follow Xander everywhere, so Brady may urge Sarah to remember that and keep herself safe from whatever threat is next.

Over with Xander, he’ll get a visit from Marlena and gladly make her his confidant.

Xander will have a therapy session from his hospital bed and discuss the good news about reuniting with Sarah.

However, Xander may admit that getting the reunion of his dreams has brought some concerns.

It could leave Xander fearing it’ll all be yanked away and that he wouldn’t survive losing Sarah once more.

Days of Our Lives spoilers say Xander may find himself waiting on the other shoe to drop, so stay tuned.
